# peddet-orin: product requirements

**Goal.** Measure a person detector, torchvision's SSDLite320 MobileNetV3-Large with its COCO weights, on a Jetson Orin Nano, at FP32, FP16 and INT8, and publish accuracy and performance numbers that can be traced to run logs and reproduced from a fresh clone within the tolerances of R-11.

**Non-goals.** Other models or datasets, multi-object tracking, a serving layer, and redistributing images, labels or weights.

**Definitions.**

- *Engine latency*: one inference, including the copies to and from device memory. *End-to-end latency* adds image decoding, preprocessing and postprocessing.
- *Peak process RSS*: the benchmark process's peak resident set size, from `resource.getrusage`. It does not fully capture memory allocated on the device.
- *System RAM rise*: the highest system-wide RAM used during timing, from `tegrastats`, minus its mean during an idle baseline just before. It includes any other activity on the board, so it is logged but not published in the table.
- *Power*: the mean of the `tegrastats` VDD_IN readings during timing, in watts. It is the whole board's input power, not the GPU's alone.
